Welcome to the Island

Sri Lanka, a tropical gem in the Indian Ocean, is home to a vibrant tapestry of cultures — where Sinhalese, Tamil, Muslim, and Burgher communities coexist, embracing Buddhist, Hindu, Islamic, and Christian traditions. Shaped by centuries of colonial rule and ancient ties to India, every corner of the island offers a glimpse into its rich and layered heritage.

✦

From the fortress rock of Sigiriya to the misty highland estates of Haputale, and from the ancient temples of Kandy to the cliffside luxury of Weligama — this 13-day cultural journey weaves together Sri Lanka’s greatest stories through its monuments, traditions, and people.

Days 01–03 · Cultural Triangle

Sigiriya — Rock, Ruins & Village Life

BD

Day 01: Arrival — Sigiriya

Ayubowan! Welcome to Sri Lanka. Upon arrival, a friendly representative will greet you as you step off the aircraft and guide you through immigration to the Silk Route Lounge. Here, you can relax, freshen up, and enjoy a refreshing cup of tea while your luggage is collected. Your designated chauffeur guide will then transfer you to Sigiriya.

Travel info: Airport — Sigiriya | 111 km | Approx. 3 hours 30 minutes

For a more exclusive and breathtaking journey, opt for a helicopter transfer — skipping the long drive while soaking in Sri Lanka’s stunning landscapes from a spectacular bird’s-eye view. Approx. 30 minutes by air.

Check in at the hotel. This evening, engage in a traditional Sri Lankan dance lesson at the Habarana Cultural Centre. Step into the rhythmic heart of Sri Lanka with an inspiring lesson led by a professional instructor, learning the expressive movements, graceful postures, and captivating rhythms that define Sri Lanka’s classical dance heritage. Participants receive a Certificate of Completion at the end of the session. Approx. 1.5 hours

Day 02: Sigiriya Rock Fortress & Village Experience

After an early breakfast, proceed to visit Sigiriya Rock Citadel — a spectacular 5th-century archaeological site built on a giant rock rising sheer and impregnable from the plains. A moat, ramparts, extensive gardens, and the frescoes of the ‘Heavenly Maidens’ ring the base of the rock. As you descend, your porter will have laid out mats and Sri Lankan delicacies at a shaded rest spot — a welcome respite after the 1,200 steps to the summit. Approx. 3 hours

Proceed to Hiriwaduna for a village experience — journey aboard a Land Master through paddy fields and jungle trails, learn about traditional cultivation, handpick fresh vegetables, glide across a serene lake on a boat ride, and enjoy a traditional Sri Lankan lunch at a local village home. Approx. 3 hours

Day 03: Polonnaruwa

After an early breakfast, proceed to Polonnaruwa — a UNESCO World Heritage Site — for a cycling exploration of its magnificent ruins, graceful statues, and timeworn monuments that once formed the heart of the Sinhalese kingdom. Your knowledgeable host will bring its stories to life through the most significant sites and hidden treasures. Approx. 3 hours

Optional: Visit Dambulla Cave Temple — built by King Walagambahu in the 1st century BC, Dambulla is the most impressive of Sri Lanka’s cave temples. The complex of five caves features over 2,000 sq. metres of painted walls and ceilings — the largest area of paintings found in the world — and over 150 images of the Buddha.

Kandy — Temples, Drums & Heritage

B

Day 04: Sigiriya — Matale — Kandy

After breakfast, check out and proceed to Kandy, en route visiting a Spice and Fruit Farm in Matale. In a quiet rural village, your planter host welcomes you with farm-fresh juice before leading a guided walk through his picturesque 15-acre hillside estate. Discover pepper, papaya, cocoa, coconut, mango, and mahogany — and hear captivating stories of Sri Lanka’s plantation heritage. Approx. 3 hours

Travel info: Sigiriya — Matale — Kandy | Approx. 1 hour 45 minutes to Matale

Continue to the Matale Heritage Centre, founded by renowned artist Ena de Silva, where Sri Lanka’s traditional batik and textile arts are beautifully preserved. Stroll through the serene gardens and observe artisans at work, bringing intricate designs to life through age-old techniques. Approx. 1 hour

Travel info: Matale — Kandy | 25 km | Approx. 1 hour

Visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic — the World Heritage Site and last royal capital of Sri Lankan kings. This evening, step to the beat of traditional music and drumming at a cultural show enhancing the rich and vibrant culture of Sri Lanka. Approx. 45 minutes

Day 05: Three Temple Loop & Traditional Drum Lesson

After an early breakfast, immerse yourself in Sri Lanka’s spiritual and architectural heritage on the Three Temple Loop. Begin at Gadaladeniya Temple (12th century), ride by tuk-tuk through lush paddy fields to Lankatilaka Temple, then conclude at Embekka Temple — home to an exquisitely carved wooden shrine and breathtaking countryside views. Approx. 2.5 hours

This afternoon, discover the heartbeat of Sri Lanka through an engaging Traditional Drum Lesson with a professional percussionist whose family has mastered this ancient art for generations. Just 20 minutes from Kandy, learn the distinct rhythms, drum types, and ceremonial meanings over a cup of Ceylon tea, before joining your teacher in a short percussion harmonisation and trying on an elaborate ceremonial headdress.

Nuwara Eliya — The City of Light

BD

Day 06: Kandy — Nuwara Eliya

Breakfast at the hotel, check out and proceed to Nuwara Eliya.

Travel info: Kandy — Nuwara Eliya | 77 km | Approx. 2 hours 45 minutes

Misty steep roads twisting through lush green blankets of tea bushes in a cool climate remind visitors that they are within the range of the renowned British colonial retreat — Nuwara Eliya. Augmented by an English countryside atmosphere, the city of light, once governed by English and Scottish planters, is still studded with colonial bungalows, hedgerows, and one of the finest 18-hole golf courses in the world.

Arrival and check in at Warwick Garden.

Day 07: Meenamma’s Tea Experience

Breakfast at the hotel. Morning at leisure.

Step into the heart of Sri Lanka’s tea country with Meena Amma — a former tea picker who warmly shares her life in the upcountry estates. Stroll through lush plantations, meet the tea-picking community, and gain insight into the hard work behind every cup. Enjoy local meals prepared in a traditional country kitchen, sip freshly hand-rolled tea, and explore the estate temple and nearby school — a charming, authentic glimpse into daily life on a Sri Lankan tea estate. Approx. 2 hours

Haputale — Thotalagala Plantation House

AI

Day 08: Scenic Train Ride — Nuwara Eliya to Haputale

Breakfast at the hotel. Check out and proceed to Ambewela Railway Station for a scenic train journey through Sri Lanka’s tea country, passing gentle hills, plantations, and small villages — a relaxed glimpse into everyday life in the hill country and its long connection to tea cultivation.

Travel info: Warwick Garden — Ambewela Station | Approx. 45 minutes by road
Train: Ambewela Station — Haputale Station | Approx. 2 hours
Transfer: Haputale Station — Thotalagala | Approx. 15 minutes

Arrive and check in at Thotalagala Plantation House. Enjoy a relaxed afternoon high tea at Thotalagala — estate-grown teas served with light savoury and sweet treats, reflecting the charm of a historic plantation home in the cool hill country air.

Day 09: At Leisure

Breakfast at the hotel. Spend the day at your leisure, enjoying the beautiful surroundings of the bungalow — cool air, sweeping valley views, and the unhurried rhythm of plantation life.

Weligama — Cape & the Fort

B

Day 10: Haputale — Weligama

Breakfast at the hotel. Check out and proceed to Weligama.

Travel info: Haputale — Weligama | 207 km | Approx. 4 hours

For a more exclusive and breathtaking journey, opt for a helicopter transfer — soaring over Sri Lanka’s stunning landscapes from a spectacular bird’s-eye view. Approx. 30 minutes by air.

Arrive and check in at Cape Weligama — a luxury cliffside resort on Sri Lanka’s southern coast offering stunning Indian Ocean views. Set across 12 acres of lush gardens, this Relais & Châteaux retreat features elegant villas, infinity pools, and world-class dining. Spend the afternoon at your leisure, making the most of the resort’s exceptional facilities.

Day 11: Galle Dutch Fort & Mask Making

Breakfast at the hotel. Leave for Galle Dutch Fort.

Travel info: Weligama — Galle | 25 km | Approx. 50 minutes

Legends & Masks: A Galle Fort Experience — Your journey begins near the historic Amangalla Hotel, where you’ll meet Mr. Liyanaarachchi, an archaeologist and expert on Sri Lanka’s fortifications. As you walk through the fort’s centuries-old streets, he will bring its past to life — tracing origins from the Portuguese era, through Dutch expansions and British influences. You’ll hear about the fort’s residents and gain rare access to restricted areas most visitors never see. After the walk, a local mask-making family from Ambalangoda will guide you through the fascinating process of mask-making — from carving the wood to painting intricate designs. Try your hand at painting your own mask to take home. Approx. 3.5 hours

Day 12: At Leisure

Breakfast at the hotel. Rest of the day at leisure.

Optional: Scent of Toddy and Cinnamon — Discover the traditional art of hand-rolling cinnamon over spiced tea, then watch a skilled tapper climb the kithul palm to draw fresh toddy. A simple yet distinctive tasting experience, both comforting and grounding. (Adults only)

Optional: Sunset Cruise — Step aboard a private catamaran for a 3-hour sunset cruise along Sri Lanka’s breathtaking southern coast, departing from Mirissa at 3:00 PM. Enjoy gourmet canapés, refreshing beverages, swimming, and stand-up paddleboarding as the sun paints the horizon in amber and rose. *Available November to April only

Before You Go

Important Notes

Poya Days
Alcohol service is restricted on religious holidays (Poya Days). Alcohol is not available for purchase at bars or restaurants on these dates. Please contact the reception for guidance during these periods.
Check-In & Check-Out
Standard hotel check-in is 14:00. Standard check-out is 12:00. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and may incur additional fees.
Belongings
Hotel management is not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged personal belongings during the tour. We recommend using in-room safes and exercising care with valuables.
Travel Times
All travel times are approximate and subject to traffic conditions, road works, and weather. Please allow flexibility in your schedule.
Gratuities
Gratuities for guides and drivers are at your discretion. We recommend presenting tips in an envelope at the end of your journey as a token of appreciation.
Dress Code
Light, breathable clothing is recommended. Comfortable walking shoes are essential. At religious sites, please dress modestly — covering from knees to elbows. Men in shorts and ladies in short skirts may not be permitted entry to some sites.
